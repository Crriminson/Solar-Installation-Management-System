## Will solar work during a power cut
An on-grid system stops during a power cut. This is a safety feature called anti-islanding: the inverter must not feed the lines while line workers may be repairing them. So on-grid gives no backup even on a sunny day. A customer who wants backup needs an off-grid or hybrid system with batteries. Tell customers this before they accept, because it is the most common misunderstanding.

## On-grid, off-grid or hybrid: which should a customer choose
On-grid costs least, has no batteries, uses net metering, and is the type the PM Surya Ghar subsidy is usually for. It suits homes with reliable grid and a high bill. Off-grid has batteries and no grid connection, suits sites with poor or no grid, costs more, and needs battery replacement over time. Hybrid works with the grid and keeps backup loads running, and costs more than on-grid. Our packages cover on-grid and off-grid from 1 kW to 5 kW.

## Does solar work in the monsoon and on cloudy days
Yes, but output drops. Panels work from daylight, not heat, and a cloudy day may give a fraction of a sunny day's units. Rain also washes off dust. Yearly figures already include bad days, so talk about monthly or yearly generation and not a single day. Off-grid customers need to plan for several cloudy days in a row.

## Does solar damage the roof or cause leaks
It should not when installed properly. On RCC roofs the structure is fixed with anchors or ballast, and penetrations need sealing. On sheet roofs the structure fixes to the purlins. The structure must suit the local wind speed. If a customer's roof is old or already leaking, raise it during the survey, because it is better to repair it before installation.

## What is the payback period for rooftop solar
Vendors commonly quote 3 to 5 years after subsidy for homes, but this depends on tariff, usage and system cost. A higher tariff means a shorter payback, and solar replaces the most expensive slab units first. To estimate: net cost ÷ yearly saving. Yearly saving = yearly units used on site or offset × tariff. Use the customer's own bill and do not promise a figure. Our quotation shows the cost.

## How long do solar panels last
Panels are usually designed for about 25 years and keep producing after that at lower output. Inverters generally need replacing sooner than panels, and batteries sooner still. Our warranties are 20 years on panels and 2 years on inverters and batteries, counted from delivery. Be careful not to mix up the expected life with the warranty.

## Is the subsidy guaranteed
No. Our quotation shows an estimate, which is not an approval or a payment. The DISCOM and the portal decide eligibility, and money reaches the customer's bank after commissioning. The customer pays our invoice in full regardless; the subsidy is not deducted from the balance unless the payment arrangement really works that way. Never say a subsidy is confirmed.

## Can a customer get a subsidy on a bigger system
A larger system does not raise the central subsidy above Rs 78,000, but it can still make sense for a higher bill. Some states add their own top-up. Check the subsidy rule for the customer's state on the quotation. Commercial and off-grid systems do not get a residential subsidy unless the rule says so.

## How net metering saves money on the bill
Units the home does not use go out to the grid and are counted by the net meter. On the bill, exported units are set against imported units, so the customer pays for the difference. Fixed charges usually remain, so the bill rarely reaches zero. Rules vary by state and DISCOM.

## Do solar panels need maintenance
Very little: regular cleaning, a check of the inverter display, and watching generation numbers. If generation drops or a fault shows, the customer raises a complaint and we open a ticket. Ask them to send a photo of the inverter display.
